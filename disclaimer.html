1. Purpose of the project

London Film Route is a web application developed as a final academic project for the Information Modelling and Web Technologies course, following the guidelines and requirements set by Professor Fabio Vitali. The project provides users with curated, one-day itineraries across London to discover real-world locations featured in famous films. To accommodate diverse audiences and preferences, the itineraries are tailored to three distinct themes—Historical, Romantic Movies, and Children's Movies—featuring varied narrative tones and flexible durations. Additionally, the application offers an interactive customization feature, allowing users to select and switch between four distinct typographic themes (English Renaissance, Early 1900s, 90s-00s Newspaper, Future) according to their aesthetic preferences. This website is intended solely for educational and portfolio purposes.

2. Sources and external services

The interactive map displaying all film locations has been implemented using Leaflet. Street View integrations for individual locations are powered by Google Maps. Each location profile includes embedded hyperlinks to YouTube to allow users to view the corresponding movie scene; the project authors assume no responsibility or liability for the removal, modification, or unavailability of these third-party videos. All descriptive information regarding the filming locations has been compiled through open research and online sources.

4. Visitor information

Before undertaking any physical or in-person visits to the locations featured in the itineraries, users are strongly advised to independently verify transportation options, accessibility, opening hours, admission costs, safety guidelines, and potential access restrictions. The proposed routes and practical details serve as interpretive and planning aids only and may not reflect real-time conditions or operational changes.
